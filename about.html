About TMA

An AI-powered product development partner for startups and growing businesses.

Why TMA

Founded by engineers who were tired of watching startups choose between agencies that move slow and freelancers who disappear after launch.

Our Story

TMA is a product engineering studio built for startups that need to move fast without compromising on quality. We're a small, senior team with 15+ years of hands-on expertise shipping production software — from early-stage MVPs to enterprise-grade platforms — across mobile, web and backend.

That means startups and growing businesses get a partner who has actually shipped and led delivery of production apps — not just a dev shop taking orders. We embed with your team, own outcomes, and bring hands-on technical leadership so you can ship fast without cutting corners.
